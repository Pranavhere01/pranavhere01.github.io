Saarthi / 2026

Saarthi · सारथी

Founder & CEO · Product, engine, and app

A personal AI astrologer grounded in an exact Vedic birth chart, with memory the user controls. I own the product from requirements and architecture to the engine, app, and report.

  • The product decision: compute the chart in code; let AI interpret the facts. The engine produces 16 divisional charts, a 120-year planetary timeline, and a citable facts sheet.
  • The build: a tested Python engine, TypeScript cloud functions, and one Flutter app for web, Android, and iOS, in English and Hindi.
